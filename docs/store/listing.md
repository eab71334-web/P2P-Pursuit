# Store listing — Hairpin Rush

## English
**Title (30):** Hairpin Rush
**Short description (80):** Race mountain hairpins solo, on LAN or online. Drift, dodge, win.
**Full description:**
Hairpin Rush is a fast mountain-rally racing game built around tight hairpin bends.

- Solo races against rivals, plus LAN co-op, team races and free-for-all
- Customise your car and paint, and unlock new cars
- Cockpit and chase cameras, car controls (lights, wipers, horn, hazards) and in-car radio
- Play with touch or a gamepad (PS4 / Xbox pads supported, buttons remappable)
- Weather, flags, spectators and detailed stages

Contains ads (optional rewarded videos). In-app coin packs available.
